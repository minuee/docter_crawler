Usage

When you visit a PubMed article, if the article has been cited by others (according to SemanticScholar), you will see a button . Click the button to get the paper's citation contexts as well as how the paper was cited by others.

When we failed to retrieve any citation contexts from SemanticScholar, or our server experiences maintenance, the button won't show up.

In addition, we also provide two services for you play with: and . The former will retrieve revelant news from EurekAlert! Science News Releases, and the latter will retrieve relevant grants from NIH and NSF.
